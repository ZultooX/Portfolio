01How I like to develop

I'm a programmer focused on gameplay, engine, graphics and tools development. I enjoy understanding how the different parts of a game work together, and I like working close to the technology to see what happens beneath the surface.

I'm especially interested in solving technical problems, experimenting with new ideas and building tools and systems that improve the development process. Whether it's gameplay, rendering or tools for other developers, I enjoy taking something from an idea to a working system.

02What I'm working on

My biggest project right now is Eclipsed Engine, a custom C++ game engine and editor I'm building with one other developer. I've written about three quarters of it, including the reflection system, hot-reloadable game code, the asset pipeline and most of the editor.

03Outside of development

I'm very interested in astronomy and love learning about the universe, from nearby planets and stars to distant galaxies. I also enjoy astrophotography, which combines that interest with the challenge of capturing and processing images of the night sky.
