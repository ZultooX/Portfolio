About ME
HI! My name is Christopher Vedlund and I'm a programmer focused on gameplay, engine, graphics and tools development. Beyond these areas, I always strive to learn new things and keep improving as a developer.


I enjoy building systems from the ground up and understanding how different parts of a game work together. I'm especially interested in solving technical problems, experimenting with new ideas and creating tools and systems that improve the development process. Whether I'm implementing gameplay, working on rendering or building tools for other developers, I enjoy taking something from an idea to a working system.


My biggest project right now is Eclipsed Engine, a custom C++ game engine and editor I'm building with one other developer.


Outside of development I'm very interested in astronomy, from nearby planets and stars to distant galaxies, and I enjoy astrophotography: capturing and processing images of objects in the night sky.
