Overview

Dino Dynasty Evolved is a stylized 3D action-adventure where you explore, fight enemies, and evolve your dinosaur herd.

I joined the project toward the end of my internship at ION Game Design, contributing primarily as a gameplay programmer. My work focused on bug fixes, checkpoints, and combat feedback systems.

This project gave me hands-on experience with Unreal Engine, C++, gameplay systems, and collaborative development in a small team.

Key Contributions

  • Bug fixes & Optimizations
  • Checkpoints
  • Combat feedback via on-hit VFX
