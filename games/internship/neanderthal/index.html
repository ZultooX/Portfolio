Neanderthal

Humanity's evolution as a species has accelerated over the last 30,000-40,000 years unparalleled in evolution of life on Earth. What triggered this change? Play as the Archaic Man (Player Green), Neanderthal (Player Yellow), or Cro-Magnon (Player Red) species fighting to survive.

Role: Game Programmer
Team: 3
Engine: Unity 2022

Overview

Neanderthal is a strategy and survival game where players guide an early human tribe through a harsh prehistoric world, focusing on survival, resource management, and adaptation to a changing environment.

I joined the project as a gameplay programmer with a focus on developing a free-to-play version of the game. My responsibilities included designing and implementing monetization systems, progression structures, and adapting gameplay features for a F2P model.

In addition, I worked on an early prototype exploring a migration of the game’s backend networking from AWS to Firebase, aiming to simplify the infrastructure and improve scalability for online features.

Key Contributions

  • Integrated AdMob for in-game monetization
  • Prototyped backend networking migration from AWS to Firebase
