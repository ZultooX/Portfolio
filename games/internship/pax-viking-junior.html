Pax Viking Junior

A charming adventure game about Viking cats exploring Europe, combining strategy, exploration, and light resource management.

Role: Game Programmer
Team: 1
Engine: Unity 2022

Overview

Pax Viking Junior is a family-friendly adventure game where players lead a crew of Viking cats across Europe, collecting treasures, making animal friends, and exploring new lands. Designed for younger audiences, it combines strategy, exploration, and light resource management in an accessible and colorful experience.

I joined the project with the goal of developing and adapting the game into a free-to-play version, focusing on implementing monetization systems, progression flow, and gameplay adjustments suitable for that format.
