Greenland

Greenland is a strategic turn-based survival game. Choose your tribe and fight to survive rival factions and the harsh and shifting conditions of Greenland. Become a friendly hunter and gatherer or a gruesome raider, the tribes fate is in your hands.

Role: Game Programmer
Team: 3
Engine: Unity 2022

Overview

Greenland is a strategic turn-based survival game developed, where players lead a tribe through harsh environmental conditions, resource scarcity, and shifting political dynamics in an evolving world.

I joined the project as a gameplay programmer with a focus on developing a free-to-play version of the game. My responsibilities included designing and implementing monetization systems, progression adjustments, and supporting gameplay features adapted for a F2P structure.

In addition, I worked on an early prototype to migrate the game’s backend networking from AWS to Firebase, aiming to simplify infrastructure and improve scalability for live features.

Key Contributions

  • Integrated AdMob for in-game monetization
  • Prototyped backend networking migration from AWS to Firebase
