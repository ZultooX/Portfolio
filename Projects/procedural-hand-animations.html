FABRIK Hand Animation
For my specialization course at The Game Assembly, I recreated Uncharted 4's procedural hand animations in my own custom graphics engine, using the FABRIK inverse kinematics algorithm.

At a glance

  • Team: Solo
  • Period: February 2025 – April 2025
  • Engine: My own custom DirectX 11 engine
  • Tech: C++, FABRIK, DirectX 11, ImGui, Premake, nlohmann/json
