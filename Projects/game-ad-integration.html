Game Ad Integration
During my internship at ION Game Design I adapted four of the studio's released games into free-to-play versions. That meant integrating Google AdMob, adjusting progression and gameplay for a free-to-play model, and prototyping a move of the online backend from AWS to Firebase.

At a glance

  • Role: Game Programmer
  • Period: October 2025 – May 2026
  • Engine: Unity 2022
  • Tech: C#, Google AdMob, Firebase, AWS, Git
  • Process: Agile, Scrum

Neanderthal

A strategy and survival game where you guide an early human tribe through a harsh prehistoric world. Team of 3.
  • Designed and implemented monetization systems and progression for the free-to-play version, and integrated AdMob.
  • Built an early prototype migrating the game's backend networking from AWS to Firebase, to simplify the infrastructure and improve scalability for online features.

Steam · App Store · Google Play

Greenland

A strategic turn-based survival game where you lead a tribe through Greenland's harsh conditions. Team of 3.
  • Designed and implemented monetization systems and progression adjustments for the free-to-play version, and integrated AdMob.
  • Worked on the same AWS to Firebase backend prototype.

Steam · App Store · Google Play

High Frontier 4 All

A digital adaptation of the board game about realistic space exploration and interplanetary expansion. Team of 8.
  • Implemented the free-to-play version of the game and integrated AdMob.

Steam · App Store · Google Play

Pax Viking Junior

A family-friendly adventure game where a crew of Viking cats explores Europe. I was the only programmer on this adaptation.
  • Adapted the game into a free-to-play version: monetization, progression flow and gameplay adjustments, with AdMob integrated for ads.

App Store · Google Play
