Finders Keepers
Travel through time and, with the power of a handy zapper, outsmart and outrun your alien pursuer.

Finders Keepers was my first group project at The Game Assembly, where we were tasked with making an infinite runner. Looking back at the code I wrote then, I can really see how much I've improved during my time at TGA.

At a glance

  • Team: 14
  • Reference: Subway Surfers
  • Duration: 7 weeks at 50% pace
  • My contributions: player movement, animations, the portal mechanic and the time travel mechanic

Player movement

My group couldn't decide how the movement should work, so I offered to build two versions for them to try. One was like Temple Run 2, where the player isn't confined to lanes and has more freedom. The other was like Subway Surfers, where the player moves between fixed lanes.

After trying both and discussing them, the group decided the lanes were a better fit for our game.
