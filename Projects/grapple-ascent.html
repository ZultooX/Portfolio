Grapple Ascent
You are a mechanically engineered yo-yo, built by a foreign civilization to scale an endlessly tall mountain. Grapple Ascent was made in a team of five for the GMTK Game Jam 2024. You can play it on itch.io.

The jam's theme was "Built to Scale". My team interpreted it as a yo-yo built to scale a mountain, and since a finite mountain is no fun, we made it infinite.

Tower generation

The tower generation is simple but dynamic. It starts by creating the main tower piece with a random orientation, within a min-max range set by the biome, so every tower feels unique.

Then flying boulders and stones are placed around the mountain, forming natural climbing paths. They make the terrain more engaging and give the player strategic routes up the tower.

The generation doesn't check whether stones end up too close together, which could in theory create an impassable section. After many players tried the game on itch.io, we concluded the chance was small enough not to noticeably affect the experience.
