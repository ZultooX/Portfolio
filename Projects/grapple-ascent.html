Grapple Ascent
You are a mechanically engineered yo-yo, built by a foreign civilization to scale an endlessly tall mountain. Grapple Ascent was made in a team of five for the GMTK Game Jam 2024. You can play it on itch.io.

At a glance

  • Role: Gameplay Programmer
  • Event: GMTK Game Jam 2024
  • Team: 5
  • Engine: Unity 2022
  • Tech: C#, procedural generation, Git

What I did

  • Procedural mountain generation: generated the mountain the yo-yo climbs.
  • Gameplay features: implemented gameplay features.
  • UI: built the game's user interface.
