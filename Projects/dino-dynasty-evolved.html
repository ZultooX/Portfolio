Dino Dynasty Evolved
Dino Dynasty Evolved is a stylized 3D action-adventure where you explore, fight enemies and evolve your dinosaur herd. It is released on Steam.

At a glance

  • Role: Gameplay Programmer
  • Studio: ION Game Design (internship)
  • Team: 30
  • Engine: Unreal Engine 5
  • Tech: C++, Blueprints, Niagara, Git

What I did

I joined the project toward the end of my internship at ION Game Design as a gameplay programmer, working in Unreal Engine 5 with C++ and Blueprints.
  • Checkpoints: worked on the game's checkpoints.
  • Combat feedback: added on-hit VFX to improve combat feel.
  • Bug fixes and optimization: fixed bugs and performance issues.

The project gave me hands-on experience with Unreal Engine, C++, gameplay systems and collaborative development.
